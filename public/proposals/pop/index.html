    What counts as a revision round?

    We work in weekly sprints, unless events or scheduling get in the way. Here's what one round looks like. We'll set the exact schedule together once the proposal is agreed.

    We design (4–5 working days) We present: live, recorded video, or PDF You send feedback (within 2–3 days)

    Keeping feedback on track

    01

    Name 1–2 point people

    Choose who leads the project on your side, and who should be in the weekly meetings.

    02

    Keep the review team consistent

    The same people giving feedback and overseeing the process from start to finish keeps decisions moving.

    03

    Bring the board in early

    If board members will review designs, involve them early, or decide up front exactly when they'll weigh in.

    Differing opinions halfway through can delay feedback rounds, pause the project, and may mean charging for extra design revisions that fall outside the agreed scope.
